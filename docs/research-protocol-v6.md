# v6: fixed-expert PPO selector

Registered before v6 validation preview and training. Historical periods were already inspected in v3/v4/v5: **retrospective exploratory development, not untouched out-of-sample evidence or a guarantee of improvement**. Preserve all v5 sources and outputs.

## Fixed design

Exactly two PPO candidates: `lognet_ppo`, reward 100 times daily log net return; `drawdown_ppo`, reward 100 times (daily log net return minus 0.5 times the increment of running maximum drawdown). Common fixed reward scale100 matches the inherited convention. No other reward penalty. Three seeds 7/17/29 × four existing folds × two objectives = **24 final checkpoints**, exactly **20,000 environment steps each**, random 126-session training episodes. Real pinned FinRL DRLAgent PPO, flat Box observation/MlpPolicy, Discrete(4) actions, 64×64 hidden network, learning rate 1e-4. Existing adapter fixes rollout=100 and batch=50; no new parameter search. Final checkpoint only.

Four fixed experts, in tie order: `cash`, `equal50`, `vol10`, `trend10`. Cash is 100% cash. Equal50 is 50% TOTAL risky exposure distributed equally across the three ETFs, with 50% cash. Vol10 and trend10 use inherited fixed 10% annual-volatility targets, 20/60-session shrunk covariance, 1% volatility floor, and 60/120-session trend gate for trend10 only. Four shadow accounts independently apply each expert using raw open execution, actual transaction costs, dividend entitlement/receivable/payment and splits. The selector sees only causal information available at the decision close, including 20-session trailing shadow net-log performance; no future-close action or future shadow values. All shadow accounts reset to cash/NAV=1 with the actual account on each segment; no hidden prehistory warm-up. Before 20 observations, use the available causal history and expose its count; empty history is zero and ties choose cash. Actual account is initialized at NAV=1 on each evaluation segment. Both actual and shadows rebalance every five trading sessions. Inactive-day proposals do not trigger trades and are logged separately from executed selection. This is a comparison of two fixed objectives, not a promise that expert selection improves performance.

## No-training comparators, validation and freeze

Six deterministic strategies, once each per fold/cost: the four individual experts, `equal_target_mix` (arithmetic mean of four contemporaneous target-weight vectors in ONE actual account, **not an average of four return series**), and `trailing20` (argmax of causal trailing-20 shadow net log performance; exact ties choose cash/equal50/vol10/trend10). All pay actual trading costs/corporate actions. No baseline training.

First produce signed validation-only baseline preview; never compute test metrics at this stage. Do not alter the registered family based on previews. Per fold choose PPO objective using mean across all three seeds of total return minus 0.5 maximum drawdown. Separately choose deployment from both PPO objective seed means and all six scalar rule scores using the same criterion. Rules are one deterministic observation, not independent seed replicates. Ties use PPO registry then rule registry. Also freeze best individual fixed expert on validation, with expert tie order. All 24 checkpoints, training records, validation ledgers and comparator validations across ALL folds must be present, hashed, reconciled and frozen **before any new test metric**. Fit shards cannot bypass global freeze.

## Evaluation and reporting

5bps one-way primary costs; 10/20bps stress replay the exact same saved models and validation choices without retraining/reselection. Report both objectives, every seed and all six rules, all folds/costs: total return, drawdown, volatility, Sharpe, turnover, actual equity exposure, requested/executed/ignored selection logs. No test winner selection. Deployment rules appear as single paths rather than repeated independent seed trials.

Primary contrasts for each PPO objective (identify validation-selected objective) are versus equal-target mix, trailing20 selector and validation-fixed best individual expert. Paired daily returns, seed-mean increments, 10-day moving-block bootstrap / 2000 draws; intervals describe annualized arithmetic excess, not CAGR or probability of future profit. Seeds are optimization randomness, not independent market histories. No multiplicity correction or confirmatory claim. Independently reconstruct every saved actual-account ledger from raw prices/actions; shadow synthetic tests cover causality, cost drag, dividends and split accounting. Preserve model zip files, signed records, freeze manifest, raw ledgers, audit sidecars, CSV metrics and contrasts.
